# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Welcome questions and constructive disagreement.
Harassment, discrimination, threats and publication of private information are
unacceptable. Discuss technical decisions on their merits and respect boundaries.

The maintainer may remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionate
to the behavior. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's "Report a vulnerability" form
(choose the lowest severity and say it is a conduct concern); they will be handled fairly and without retaliation.
